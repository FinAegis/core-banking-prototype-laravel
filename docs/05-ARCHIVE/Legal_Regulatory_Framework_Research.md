# Legal & Regulatory Framework Research
## GDFS Implementation Research - Phase 1

---

## Executive Summary

This research provides a comprehensive analysis of the legal and regulatory requirements for implementing the Global Digital Financial System (GDFS) across key jurisdictions. The findings include licensing requirements, capital thresholds, compliance frameworks, and strategic recommendations for regulatory approach.

---

## Key Jurisdictions Analysis

### 1. Germany (BaFin) - Federal Financial Supervisory Authority

#### Licensing Requirements
- **License Type**: Payment Institution license under PSD2/ZAG (Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz)
- **Minimum Capital**: €125,000 initial capital requirement
- **Approval Timeline**: 6-12 months processing period
- **Regulatory Framework**: EU Payment Services Directive 2 (PSD2) compliance mandatory

#### Key Compliance Requirements
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) implementation
- Open Banking compliance with XS2A (Access to Account) standards
- PCI DSS Level 1 certification for payment processing
- GDPR compliance with data protection by design principles

### 2. United States - Multi-State Regulatory Framework

#### Federal Requirements
- **MSB Registration**: Money Services Business registration with FinCEN
- **Bank Secrecy Act**: Full AML/CTF compliance framework
- **OFAC Compliance**: Real-time sanctions screening implementation

#### State-Level Licensing
- **Money Transmitter License**: Required in 47+ states
- **Capital Requirements**: $5,000 - $500,000 per state (varies significantly)
- **Bond Requirements**: $10,000 - $2,000,000 per state
- **Timeline**: 6-18 months per state license

#### Regulatory Complexity
- 50+ separate regulatory frameworks to navigate
- Ongoing compliance costs: $2-5M annually
- Legal complexity requires specialized regulatory counsel

### 3. United Kingdom (FCA) - Financial Conduct Authority

#### Authorization Framework
- **License Type**: Payment Institution authorization under Payment Services Regulations 2017
- **Minimum Capital**: £125,000 initial capital requirement
- **Prudential Requirements**: Ongoing capital adequacy monitoring

#### Open Banking Compliance
- **API Standards**: Implementation of Open Banking UK standards
- **Security Framework**: FAPI (Financial-grade API) compliance
- **Consumer Protection**: Strong Customer Authentication requirements

### 4. Lithuania - Bank of Lithuania (EU Entry via Paysera Partnership)

#### Strategic Advantages
- **Existing EMI License**: Leverage Paysera's established Electronic Money Institution license
- **Immediate EU Access**: Direct access to all 27 EU member states through existing passporting rights
- **No Capital Requirements**: No additional licensing costs required through partnership
- **Operational Infrastructure**: Established regulatory relationship and compliance framework

#### Partnership Benefits
- **PSD2 Compliance**: Immediate access to established European payment services framework
- **GDPR Implementation**: Existing EU data protection compliance infrastructure
- **MiCA Preparation**: Ready integration with upcoming crypto-asset regulations
