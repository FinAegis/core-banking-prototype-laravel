Why Regulators Love GCU

Money stays in regulated banks, all users KYC verified, full transaction visibility. Works within existing banking framework - no disruption, only enhancement.

Perfect Regulatory Compliance by Design

Money Never Leaves Banking System

Unlike crypto, customer funds stay in regulated banks (Santander, Deutsche Bank, Chase). Each bank maintains standard compliance for their portion. No new regulatory framework needed.

  • Standard bank deposits with full regulatory oversight
  • Immediate Bank of Lithuania oversight and supervision
  • Established EU PSD2 and EMD2 compliance framework
  • Immediate GCU issuance capability with 110% initial backing

Phase 2: EU Passport Activation

  • Enhanced capital requirements and oversight

Phase 3: Global Expansion

Systematic expansion to major global markets with dedicated regulatory applications and central bank partnerships for worldwide GCU adoption.
