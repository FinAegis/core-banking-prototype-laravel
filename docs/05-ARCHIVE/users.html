Frequently Asked Questions

How is GCU different from other stablecoins? +
GCU is the first user-controlled currency basket. Unlike USDC or USDT (pegged to USD only), GCU holders vote on the composition of major currencies and gold. This provides diversification and gives users democratic control over their money's backing.
What are the conversion fees? +
GCU charges only the minimal spread between buy/sell rates (0.01-0.05%). This is much lower than traditional banks (2-4%) or even most crypto exchanges (0.1-1%). The system earns only from this small spread, with no hidden fees.
How secure are my funds? +
Every GCU token is backed 1:1 by real assets held in regulated banks and custodians. We provide real-time blockchain proof of reserves, third-party audits, and full regulatory compliance with EU banking laws.
How does voting work? +
Each GCU token represents one vote. Holders can vote on the currency basket composition monthly. Voting is weighted by holdings, and the results determine how new reserves are allocated and existing reserves are rebalanced.
Can I spend GCU anywhere? +
You can spend GCU anywhere our payment network is accepted, which will initially include major merchants and ATMs. GCU automatically converts to the local currency at the point of sale using real-time exchange rates.
