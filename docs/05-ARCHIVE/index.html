How It Works

Simple 3-step process

1

Choose Your Banks

Select which banks store your money: 40% Paysera, 30% Deutsche Bank, 30% Santander. Each bank only sees their portion.

2

Get Deposit Insurance

Your funds are protected: €100,000 per EU bank, $250,000 per US bank. Government insurance covers bank failures.

3

Vote & Earn

Vote on currency composition monthly. Banks can even pay you interest on deposits if you allow lending.
