About GCU: User-Controlled Global Currency

Building the first user-controlled global currency where holders vote on composition. Real assets, transparent backing, minimal fees.

Where Your Money Actually Lives

🏦 Your Money Stays in Real Banks

Key Innovation: When you buy GCU, your money doesn't disappear into a blockchain. It stays in regulated banks YOU choose - Santander in Spain, Deutsche Bank in Germany, Chase in USA. Each bank only sees and controls the portion you allocate there. Your funds remain protected by deposit insurance in each country.

🛡️ Multi-Bank Security Architecture

Revolutionary Approach: Unlike Bitcoin's anonymous chaos or single-company stablecoins, GCU uses identified accounts in multiple banks:

  • Distributed Risk: If one bank fails, only that portion is affected - and it's covered by that country's deposit insurance
  • Real Deposit Protection: €100,000 in EU, $250,000 in USA per bank - your money is insured
  • Bank Privacy: Each bank only sees their portion of your funds, not your total wealth
  • GDPR Compliance: Full data protection for identified users, unlike anonymous crypto

Revolutionary Multi-Bank Architecture

                
                

                    

                        
1

                        
Choose Your Banks

                        
Select which regulated banks to store your funds: Santander, Deutsche Bank, Chase, HSBC, etc. You decide the distribution - maybe 40% in Germany, 30% in USA, 30% in UK. Each bank only knows about their portion.

                    

                    
                    

                        
2

                        
Deposit Insurance Protection

                        
Your funds are protected by each country's deposit insurance: €100,000 in EU banks, $250,000 in US banks, £85,000 in UK banks. If a bank fails, government insurance covers your deposits.

                    

                    
                    

                        
3

                        
Banks Can Even Pay You Interest

                        
If you allow banks to use your deposits for lending, you earn interest just like regular savings. Your money works for you while backing your GCU tokens. No locked funds sitting idle.

                    

                    
                    

                        
4

                        
Identified Users Only

                        
Full KYC/AML compliance means no anonymous criminals or money laundering. Banks, regulators, and you know exactly who's in the system. GDPR protects your data while ensuring system integrity.

🌐 Multi-Bank Resilience

                    

                        Unlike traditional systems where one bank outage stops everything, GCU operates through multiple banks running independent servers.

✓ Bank Offline?

                            
Other banks continue operating. Access your funds through any working bank.

                        

                        

                            
✓ Server Issues?

                            
Multiple servers ensure system availability. No single point of failure.

                        

                        

                            
✓ Regional Problems?

                            
Banks in other regions continue normally. Geographic distribution protects you.
