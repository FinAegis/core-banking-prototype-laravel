# GCU Business Model & Economics

**Sustainable Revenue Model for User-Controlled Global Currency**

## 💰 Revenue Streams

### Primary Revenue: Conversion Fees (0.01%)
- **Source**: Currency conversions within GCU ecosystem
- **Rate**: 0.01% per conversion (vs 2-4% traditional banking)
- **Volume**: Projected 1M+ conversions per month at scale
- **Annual Revenue**: $12M+ at 1B monthly conversion volume

### Secondary Revenue: Banking Partnerships
- **Revenue Share**: 0.05% of deposits held at partner banks
- **Bank Benefits**: Compliant, verified customers with deposit guarantees
- **Interest Earnings**: Banks can lend deposits, share interest with GCU holders
- **Partnership Revenue**: $50M+ annual at $10B assets under management

### Tertiary Revenue: API & Integration Fees
- **Business API**: $0.001 per transaction for business integrations
- **White Label**: Platform licensing for other financial institutions
- **Premium Features**: Advanced analytics, priority support
- **Enterprise Revenue**: $10M+ annual from business customers

## 🏛️ Stakeholder Value Proposition

### For Users
- **Cost Savings**: 99%+ reduction in FX fees ($200 savings per $10K conversion)
- **Inflation Protection**: Diversified currency exposure vs single-currency erosion
- **Democratic Control**: Vote on currency allocation vs bank/central bank decisions
- **Security**: Multi-bank distribution + government insurance
- **Interest Earnings**: Benefit from bank lending programs

### For Banks
- **New Revenue Stream**: Earn from GCU deposit management
- **Compliant Customers**: Pre-verified KYC/AML users only
- **Deposit Growth**: Stable, long-term deposit relationships
- **No Crypto Risk**: Traditional fiat currency operations only
- **Lending Opportunities**: Use deposits for traditional banking products

### For Regulators
- **Full Transparency**: Complete audit trail of all transactions
- **KYC Compliance**: No anonymous users, full identification
- **Existing Framework**: Works within traditional banking regulations
- **Stability**: Reduces reliance on single-currency systems
- **Innovation**: Supports fintech innovation within regulatory bounds

### For Investors
- **Scalable Model**: Network effects and viral growth
- **Defensive Moat**: Regulatory approval + bank partnerships
- **Large Market**: $2.5T addressable market with clear segments
- **Recurring Revenue**: Predictable conversion volume
- **Exit Strategy**: IPO or acquisition by major financial institution
